============================================================ 4. WHY
KNITTED TEXTILES
============================================================

Knitted fabrics are formed from interconnected yarn loops.

The documents describe knitted textiles as offering characteristics such
as: - Natural stretch and recovery - Flexibility - Soft and comfortable
hand feel - Drape and conformability - Breathability - Surface dimension
and texture - Adaptability across different weights and structures -
Freedom for engineered functional constructions

These characteristics can make knitted textiles particularly suitable
for: - Bedding - Mattress covers - Mattress ticking - Fitted sheets -
Blankets - Throws - Comfort layers - Upholstery - Healthcare textiles -
Selected technical applications

ACTIF’s stated reason for focusing on knitting: Modern products may need
textiles that adapt to users, mattresses, contours and product forms.
Knitted construction gives product developers flexibility to engineer
comfort, elasticity, breathability, texture and functional performance.

============================================================ 5. KNITTED
VS WOVEN — CUSTOMER EXPLANATION
============================================================

Use this explanation when a customer asks about knitted versus woven
fabric.

KNITTED: - Construction: interconnected yarn loops - Typical feel: soft
and flexible - Natural stretch: generally higher - Conformability:
adapts more easily to contours - Drape: fluid and relaxed - Surface:
dimensional and versatile - Strong fit: comfort and adaptability

WOVEN: - Construction: warp and weft yarns interlaced at right angles -
Typical feel: firm and structured - Natural stretch: generally lower
unless stretch yarns/special constructions are used - Dimensional
character: suited to rigidity and controlled shape - Surface: often
flatter and more structured - Strong fit: stability and structure

Important: Neither construction is universally better. The right
construction depends on the final product and required performance.

For bedding, the documents emphasize that knitted textiles can adapt to
mattress depth, movement and contours while providing a soft sleeping
surface.

============================================================ 15. CUSTOM
TEXTILE DEVELOPMENT PROCESS
============================================================

ACTIF’s development process is described as:

01 — APPLICATION Understand where and how the textile will be used.

02 — MATERIAL Select the appropriate fibre and yarn combination.

03 — CONSTRUCTION Develop the knitted structure around product
requirements.

04 — SPECIFICATION / PHYSICAL PARAMETERS Define parameters such as yarn
count, gauge, GSM, width, stretch, recovery and thickness.

05 — AESTHETICS Develop colour, texture, surface character, softness and
hand feel.

06 — PERFORMANCE Define required performance and, where appropriate,
functional finishing.

07 — VALIDATION Evaluate the textile against agreed specifications and
relevant testing requirements.

08 — SCALE / COMMERCIAL PRODUCTION Move an approved development toward
repeatable commercial production.

The overall progression is: CONCEPT → DEVELOPMENT → APPROVAL →
PRODUCTION

Another source summarizes the approach as: UNDERSTAND THE PRODUCT →
ENGINEER THE TEXTILE → DELIVER IT CONSISTENTLY
